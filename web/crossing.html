The goal

The harder crossing: TWO rivers, a rocky island between them, and the flag on the far bank. Bridge the near river first, then ferry materials across it - one of the far bridge's heavy beams is still on this bank and needs two carriers over bridge one - then build the far bridge and bring everyone over. Six pieces, three of them heavy, four miis. A new layout is generated each puzzle. By default a built-in assigner gives jobs by who's free and closest. Connect your own model (OpenAI key or your Ollama) and every mii decides for itself instead - claiming work, asking for a lift partner, answering or declining each other's asks. Same rules, same reachability, the miis still do the lifting.
