The goal

Bring all 4 supply crates from the grove back to camp. The known detour around the ridge is a long walk; somewhere in the ridge is a hidden pass nobody knows about. Connect your own model (OpenAI key or your Ollama) and every mii decides for itself: fetch, scout, share what it found - or keep it to itself. Without a model, built-in logic simply takes the long way and never scouts or shares. A new layout is generated each puzzle; Restart replays this one so you can compare runs (for example sharing on vs off - a descriptive comparison, not a controlled experiment).
