The goal

Bring all 4 supply crates from the warrens back to camp. The stone door opens only while both pressure plates are held, so two miis must keep holding while carriers shuttle crates through - if a holder leaves early, the door slams shut on anyone mid-trip. A hidden crack (in most layouts) leads to a golden cache crate; connect your own model (OpenAI key or your Ollama) and every mii decides for itself: fetch, hold, release, scout, share what it found - or keep it to itself. Without a model, built-in logic cooperates by assignment but never scouts or shares. Every layout is generated and checked for solvability; Restart replays this one so you can compare runs (a descriptive comparison, not a controlled experiment).
